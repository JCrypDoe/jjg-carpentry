Who We Are

Built on Hard Work & Honest Service

JJG Carpentry LLC was founded in Miami with a simple idea: South Florida homeowners deserve a handyman they can actually trust. No surprise charges, no shoddy work, no excuses — just skilled tradespeople who show up on time and get the job done right.

What started as a one-man carpentry operation has grown into a full-service home improvement team covering everything from plumbing and electrical to pressure cleaning and roof repairs. We've completed over 500 jobs across Miami-Dade and Broward County, and every single one has been backed by our satisfaction guarantee.

We're not a big corporate company — we're your neighbors. We live here, work here, and take pride in keeping South Florida homes in top shape.
